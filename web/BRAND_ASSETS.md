# Source platform logos

Original brand assets, bundled locally and displayed without recoloring or distortion.

- Confluence and Jira: icon SVGs from the [Atlassian logo library](https://atlassian.design/foundations/logos), downloaded from `https://atlassian.design/assets/8601660886bc/logos/confluence_app.zip` and `https://atlassian.design/assets/8601660886bc/logos/jira_app.zip`.
- Slack: official [media kit](https://slack.com/media-kit) and [navigation icon SVG](https://a.slack-edge.com/9cc0056/marketing/img/nav/logo.svg).
- Google Drive: [brand guidelines](https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/branding) and its linked [2026 product logo](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/drive_2026/v2/web-64dp/logo_drive_2026_color_2x_web_64dp.png).

Retrieved 2026-10-17. Marks belong to their respective owners and identify the connected platforms.
